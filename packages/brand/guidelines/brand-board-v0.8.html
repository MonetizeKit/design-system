Ground rules

One ground per section, one texture per ground, shapes in the margins only. Shapes sit at low opacity behind content, never under body copy, and never inside a panel. On coloured grounds, shapes go white at low alpha; on light grounds, black. Sections are always separated by a 3px black rule.

04 · Angle

Twelve degrees

The tilt isn't loose or random. Measured off the live site, the badge and every icon tile sit at exactly 12°, alternating direction so no two neighbours lean the same way. It is a constant, not a range.

−12° · default
+12° · alternate
−1.5° · large panel
0° · text & layout
Angle spec

Small objects: ±12°, alternating. Badge, icon tiles, window dots, social tiles, stickers. Large panels: −2° to +2°. Console windows and stat cards get a whisper of rotation so they read hand-placed. Text, grids, and section rules: 0°. Never tilt body copy or a whole layout. Hover straightens a tile to 0° — the interaction is the object settling into place. On narrow screens, large panels sit square: a rotated panel's bounding box grows past the viewport edge, so panel tilt is a desktop refinement. Small-object tilt stays at ±12° everywhere — that's the signature.

12 · Dark Mode

The structure inverts

Dark mode is not a dimmed light mode. The whole system rests on a black edge and a black hard shadow, and both vanish on a dark ground — so the structure inverts. Edges become cream, shadows become a dimmed cream so the lit edge still reads brighter than the shadow behind it, and grounds drop to a warm near-black that echoes the warm cream. Use the toggle at the top to switch the whole board.

Dark mode rules

Saturated brand grounds do not invert. The orange and yellow colour blocks stay exactly as they are in both modes and re-pin their neutrals locally, so a yellow section is a light-mode island on a dark page. That keeps the brand's punch and avoids muddy deepened oranges. Anything sitting on a light or saturated fill keeps a black edge and black text — badge, icon tiles, status pills, stat cards, window dots, social tiles. Only the neutral grounds (cream, paper, lavender) and the structure on them invert. Accent text lifts: violet moves to #BB86FC so code tokens stay legible on the dark surface.
